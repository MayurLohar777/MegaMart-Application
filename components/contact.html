FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about MegaMart

How can I track my order?
Log into your MegaMart account, go to "My Orders", and click the tracking link. You'll also receive real-time SMS/email updates.
What is your return policy?
7-day hassle-free return policy. Items must be unused in original packaging. Damaged items qualify for free returns.
How long does delivery take?
Same-day delivery in metros, 2-4 business days for other cities. Premium members get guaranteed next-day delivery.
Is my payment secure?
Absolutely! We use SSL encryption and PCI-compliant payment gateways. Your financial data is never stored on our servers.
